Skim hero concepts

Ideas to replace the flowchart at the top of the Skim page. A–D keep the hand-drawn style from the rest of the site. E–H don't.

Hand-drawn

A. Skim the page

A cluttered recipe blog (photo, filler text, ad) with the useful lines highlighted in red, pulled out onto a clean recipe card. Plays on the name and explains the app in one look.

B. From anywhere into one place

A video, a reel and a website all flowing into one phone library, with the new recipe circled in red. Tells the "my mom lost recipes everywhere" story.

C. In the kitchen

A phone propped next to a steaming pot, showing one cooking step and a timer. More illustration than diagram, and backs up "people cook with their phone, not a laptop."

D. On the fridge

A week's meal plan and shopping list held on a fridge with magnets. Your picks in pencil, the generator's picks in red pen. Focuses on the planning side instead of the import.

Not hand-drawn

E. Flat product illustration

A clean vector phone showing a recipe card, with a video card flying in and a week-grid card on the side. Looks like an app landing page. Stands out from the sketches on purpose, which could feel off-brand on this site.

F. Just type

No picture at all: a big serif line in the site's heading font. Cheap to make, very confident, and works in dark mode for free. The copy carries all the weight.

G. Animated skim

Real-looking blog text where the filler fades and blurs out on a loop, leaving the ingredients highlighted. Same idea as A, but it moves, so it shows the app working instead of describing it.
